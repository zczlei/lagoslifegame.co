<h2>Link not working? Work down this list</h2>

<p>These are ordered by how often they are the actual cause.</p>

<h3>1. It stutters or lags — the game is not broken</h3>
<p>The single most reported problem is <strong>lag, not a queue</strong>. This is a shared, real-time world where tens of thousands of players sit in the same rooms, so bandwidth strains at peak hours and the world judders. That is load on the world, not your Wi-Fi being useless. Reload once, or come back off-peak. If a genuine server-full screen appears, it is temporary and retrying is the only action.</p>

<h3>2. The link opens dead from TikTok or Instagram</h3>
<p>In-app browsers sandbox or strip what the game needs. Copy the URL, open Chrome or Safari, paste it. <strong>This one fix accounts for a large share of "link not working" complaints</strong>, because almost everyone arrives from a social feed.</p>

<h3>3. Sign-up loops back on itself</h3>
<p>The game keeps you signed in with a cookie. If cookies are blocked for that domain, the login never completes and you bounce back to the start screen. Allow cookies for the game's domain (essential only is fine — it states it uses no ad trackers), or try a standard browser window rather than private mode.</p>

<h3>4. You lost your character after clearing browsing data</h3>
<p>Because login is cookie-based, wiping site data wipes your session. Keep one browser profile for the game and never clear that site's data. If you registered with an email, your account is recoverable; if you did not, treat the character as tied to that device and browser.</p>

<h3>5. It still won't open</h3>
