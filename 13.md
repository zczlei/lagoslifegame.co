<h1>Lagos Life Updates: a Dated Change Log Since Launch</h1>

<p>Lagos Life has no changelog. No patch notes, no version screen, no release feed — and its own <a href="https://lagoslife.app/terms" rel="nofollow">Terms of Use (§7)</a> let the developer adjust <strong>prices, salaries, payouts</strong> and other economy values without telling anyone. Which means a number you read anywhere, including here, is a snapshot of something that moves.</p>

<p>So this page is the record. Newest first. <strong>Nothing gets deleted</strong> — if a later check contradicts an entry, the entry stays with an addendum showing what changed and when we noticed.</p>

<div class="box">
  <strong>Three evidence bands, labelled on every line</strong>
  <p><span class="b1">PRIMARY</span> official Lagos Life page or document, read by us, with the date we read it. You can verify it yourself in under a minute — we always tell you how.</p>
  <p><span class="b2">DATED PRESS</span> Nigerian media quoting the creator or screenshotting the official counter. Carries its own date. Numbers are the creator's, not audited.</p>
  <p><span class="b3">UNVERIFIED</span> something reported by another fan guide, or announced in a post we cannot date. Stays in the log flagged, and moves up a band only when we check it in-game ourselves.</p>
</div>

<h2>Check it yourself in 20 seconds</h2>

<p>Everything above is reproducible. That is the point — you do not have to trust a fan blog about a game nobody documents.</p>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Did they ship today?</strong> Open <code>view-source:lagoslife.app</code> in a desktop browser, search for <code>build</code>. The number you find is the deploy identifier. If it is not <code>1791631002</code>, something shipped since 10 October 11:16 UTC. Do the same on <code>view-source:lagoslife.app/stats</code> — the two should match, because the whole site deploys together.</li>
  <li><strong>How many people are playing?</strong> Open <a href="https://lagoslife.app/stats" rel="nofollow">lagoslife.app/stats</a>. It states the figures refresh every 30 seconds. <strong>Numbers only appear in a live browser</strong> — a saved copy of that page shows dashes, which is why we keep screenshots with timestamps rather than archives.</li>
  <li><strong>Where do announcements actually happen?</strong> X: <a href="https://x.com/Shalom_HeyEliy" rel="nofollow">@Shalom_HeyEliy</a> (the creator, credited on the official stats page) and <a href="https://x.com/LagosLifeApp" rel="nofollow">@LagosLifeApp</a> (official account, linked from the same page). No newsletter exists.</li>
  <li><strong>Anything new in the world that nobody announced?</strong> The stats page sells ten ad slots at <strong>₦150,000 per 7 days</strong>. Brand placements are the quiet reason objects and venues appear.</li>
</ol>

<h2>What this log will not do</h2>

<p>No rumours from comment sections. No screenshot without a date. No rewriting an entry to pretend we always knew — a wrong line gets an addendum above it. And no deleting: a change log that edits its own history is a blog post.</p>

<h2>Frequently asked</h2>

<p class="q">Does Lagos Life have a changelog?</p>
<p>No. There is no changelog page on the official site and no version shown to players. The Terms let economy values change without notice (§7), which is why a dated third-party record is currently the only public change history.</p>

<p class="q">Is there a Lagos Life version number?</p>
<p>Not one players see. A build identifier sits in the page source and, on 10 October 2026, the same value appeared on the homepage and the stats page — the whole site deploys at once. When it changes, something shipped, announced or not.</p>

<p class="q">Where do updates get published?</p>
<p>On X — <strong>@Shalom_HeyEliy</strong> and <strong>@LagosLifeApp</strong>. City expansion and player milestones were both announced there and quoted by Nigerian press. There is no email list and no in-game patch-note screen.</p>

<p class="q">What is the current player count?</p>
<p>Only ever as of a timestamp. The official counter publishes players online now, active today and yesterday, total players, and visits today and all-time, refreshed every 30 seconds. Dated readings we can cite include 1,173,086 total with 55,083 online on 5 October 2026, and a reported 2 million plus on 7 October.</p>

<p class="q">Did Lagos Life add Abuja and Port Harcourt?</p>
<p>Yes — announced on X on 6 October 2026 and reported by Vanguard on 7 October. The Terms of Use already carried a section headed "One Game World, Multiple Locations" at launch on 1 October, so the multi-city world was planned, not improvised.</p>

<p class="q">Do numbers in Lagos Life guides go out of date?</p>
<p>Frequently. Prices, salaries and payouts are adjustable under §7, and the first map expansion landed on day six. Any guide printing a weekly wage without a checked-on date is describing a moving target.</p>
