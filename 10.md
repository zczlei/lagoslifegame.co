<h2>The one rule before you read the table</h2>

<p>Two fields on a career card mean different things, and mixing them up is how people pick a bad job:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>₦ per shift</strong> — the amount advertised for one shift. All 33 cards state 1-hour shifts, any time.</li>
  <li><strong>Work days per week</strong> — a schedule field (4, 5 or 6), <em>not</em> a pay unit.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why ₦21,000 a shift is not the top job</h2>

<p>Medicine → House Officer has the biggest number on any card: ₦21,000 per shift. But it is a five-day schedule, so ₦105,000 a week — <strong>below</strong> two six-day roles paying ₦18,000 a shift. The same trap at the bottom: DJ → Hype Man shows ₦8,300, the same as Teaching → Lesson Teacher, yet teaching is ₦8,300 a week higher because it runs five days, not four.</p>

<p>The lesson is worth carrying into every future patch: <strong>on Lagos Life cards, working days are a pay column disguised as a schedule.</strong></p>

<h2>Top-ups: what you are actually buying</h2>

<p>Optional naira are purchased through <strong>Flutterwave</strong> (Terms §6). Three things worth reading before you pay:</p>
<ol>
  <li><strong>It cannot come back out.</strong> Terms §5 is explicit: Game Naira is fictional, is not legal tender, cannot be withdrawn to a bank account, cannot be redeemed for real money, and selling it for real money or operating an exchange is prohibited.</li>
  <li><strong>It is a game mechanic, not an investment.</strong> Terms §8 frames economic and investment systems as mechanics.</li>
  <li><strong>Refunds are governed by §10</strong>, and payments made before 6 October 2026 ran through a different processor (Bachs) — relevant if you ever need to trace an old charge. <!-- [核实] 官方退款条款的原文口径以 terms 为准；要写细就引 §10。 --></li>
</ol>
<p>Spending real money to skip the wage ladder is a legitimate choice — but do it knowing <strong>there is no exit and no cash value</strong>. A player asking "can I earn real money from Lagos Life?" has already met the scam version of this game; the real answer is the sentence above.</p>

<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>

<h3>How do you earn virtual naira in Lagos Life?</h3>
<p>Take a route from Phone → Jobs, which listed 33 career cards on 8 October 2026. Each shows a starting role, Game Naira per shift and working days per week. Weekly pay is shift pay × working days.</p>

<h3>Which job pays the most per week?</h3>
<p>Among starting cards: Newsroom Reporter (comedy 3) and Estate Agent (charisma 3), both ₦18,000 × 6 days = <strong>₦108,000</strong>. House Officer has the biggest per-shift number at ₦21,000 but only five days, so ₦105,000 a week — below two six-day roles paying ₦18,000. Working days behave like a pay column.</p>

<h3>What is the best Lagos Life job with no skill requirement?</h3>
<p>Among cards showing no skill threshold <em>and</em> a confirmed venue: Airport Staff, Check-in Agent at ₦12,000 × 5 days = ₦60,000. <h3>Can you cash out Lagos Life naira?</h3>

<p>Yes, at the operator's discretion — Terms §7 reserves adjustment of salaries, payouts, prices, tax rates, skill requirements, asset values and job requirements. Every figure here is dated to the 8 October 2026 card set.</p>

<h2>The move-in charge: two weeks of rent, plus one week of fees</h2>

<p>Home detail panels describe the move-in charge as <strong>two weeks of rent prepaid plus one week of agent and agreement fees</strong>. <h2>Two questions people ask that the game answers with "no"</h2>

<h3>Is a Lagos Life house a real asset?</h3>
<p>No. Terms §7 places property inside the simulated economy, §8 identifies economic and investment systems as game mechanics, and §5 states Game Naira cannot be withdrawn or redeemed and has no real cash value. Selling virtual property for real money is prohibited. Anyone offering to buy your Banana Island mansion for naira is either a scam or a Terms violation.</p>

<h3>What happens to my home if I delete my account?</h3>
<p>Do not expect it gone. The Privacy Policy includes properties, virtual assets and game progress among the data it processes (§2), and account deletion is described as <strong>soft deletion</strong>: the account becomes inaccessible while records may be retained for transaction ledgers, fraud and abuse prevention, payment reconciliation, chargebacks, moderation history and legal compliance (§§10–12). Full breakdown: <a href="/is-lagos-life-safe/">is Lagos Life safe?</a></p>

<h3>Can you sell a house in Lagos Life?</h3>
<p>A resale is displayed, not confirmed. The Mushin detail showed a resale value of ₦90,400 against its ₦113,000 buy price — exactly 80% — but no resale was completed in the observation, so we would not describe 80% as the game's resale rate. Either way, §5 means the proceeds are Game Naira with no real-world value.</p>

<h3>Which is better: renting or buying out right?</h3>
<p>On the displayed prices, buying to live in costs roughly 47–61 times that home's weekly rent (our arithmetic). So renting wins until you have close to sixty weeks of that home's rent in spare cash — cash that would earn more as a career or Invest decision while you are still on a starting wage.</p>
