<h1>How to Play Lagos Life on Your Phone: The First 30 Minutes</h1>

<p><strong>Almost everybody plays this on a phone</strong> — and the official site is built to prove it: the viewport is locked with <code>viewport-fit=cover</code>, it ships a home-screen app manifest, it detects when you arrived from TikTok or Instagram, and it re-flows the screen when you turn the phone sideways. So this guide is not a desktop walkthrough with "also works on mobile" bolted on. It is a 30-minute run with the phone settings done first.</p>

<div class="box">
  <strong>Play free, in the browser:</strong> <a href="https://lagoslife.app/" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">lagoslife.app</a> — no download, no APK, no waitlist.
  <p class="mt">Can't get in? Read <a href="/lagos-life-game-link/">the Lagos Life game link and fixes</a> first. Phone details are below.</p>
</div>

<h2>Why 30 minutes, not an hour</h2>
<p>The community guides describe a <em>first hour</em>, and honestly that is a desktop-shaped number. On a phone you have a battery, a hot hand, and a feed of clips you copied a link from. Thirty minutes is what you can actually spend before you either get paid or give up — and it is enough: sign up, be born, work one shift, decide where to sleep before Saturday. Everything after that is a different game.</p>
<p>The full economy is genuinely deep — <strong>14 careers</strong>, <strong>5 housing tiers</strong>, <strong>21 venues with their own opening hours</strong>, <strong>6 needs</strong> draining on separate clocks, land, taxes, a live governor election. We are not covering that here on purpose. Use <a href="/virtual-naira/">the naira guide</a> and <a href="/homes/">the rent page</a> when you are in.</p>

<h2>Set the phone up before you sign up</h2>

<h3>1. The system floor is real, and iPhone users cannot dodge it</h3>
<p>Lagos Life does not run on old software, and it tells you so itself. Its boot check shows one of two messages verbatim:</p>
<ul class="tl">
  <li><strong>iPhone:</strong> "Lagos Life needs iOS 16.4 or newer. Go to Settings → General → Software Update, then open the game again. (Every browser on iPhone uses the same engine, so another browser won't help.)"</li>
  <li><strong>Android:</strong> update Chrome from the Play Store, then reopen the game.</li>
</ul>
<p>That iOS note is the single most useful thing on this page: <strong>on iPhone, switching to Firefox, Brave or Edge does nothing</strong> — they are all WebKit underneath. Only updating iOS fixes it. If a friend says "just use a different browser", they are wrong.</p>

<h3>2. Never tap through from TikTok or Instagram</h3>
<p>The official code checks your browser string for Twitter, Instagram, Facebook, Line, TikTok, Snapchat and LinkedIn and switches the page into a special mode when it finds one. Those in-app browsers sandbox the storage the game needs to keep you signed in. <strong>Copy the link, open Chrome or Safari, paste it.</strong> This one habit removes most "Lagos Life won't load" reports — see <a href="/lagos-life-game-link/">the link page</a> for the rest.</p>

<h3>3. Install it to the home screen (this is the "download" everyone wants)</h3>
<p>Searches for a <em>Lagos Life download</em>, <em>app</em> or <em>APK</em> are all searching for the thing below. There is no APK and no store listing — anyone offering one is not distributing the game. But the site declares a web-app manifest and a 180×180 home-screen icon, so you can make it look and feel like an app in 20 seconds:</p>
<table>
  <thead><tr><th>Phone</th><th>Add Lagos Life to the home screen</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>iPhone (iOS 16.4+)</td><td>Open <code>lagoslife.app</code> in Safari → <strong>Share</strong> → <strong>Add to Home Screen</strong> → Add. Launch it from that icon: it opens full-screen with the status bar blended in, no Safari bar.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Android</td><td>Open <code>lagoslife.app</code> in Chrome → tap <strong>⋮</strong> → <strong>Add to Home screen</strong> or <strong>Install app</strong> → Add.</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
<p class="mt"><strong>Why this matters beyond convenience:</strong> the game has code that behaves differently when it is launched from the home-screen icon rather than a browser tab — it sizes the map to the screen and to landscape orientation. The notch and the browser bar are why the buttons feel cramped in a tab. <!-- [核实] 在 iOS 16+ 与一台安卓上分别走一遍 Share → Add to Home Screen，确认菜单文案在你手上这台没变；改动写进日期 --></p>

<div class="box warn">
  <p><strong>Keep cookies allowed for the game.</strong> Login is cookie-based and stored on the device. Two habits will lose you your character: clearing that site's browsing data, and using private/incognito windows. It's also why you should not sign in as your account on a friend's phone and then let them clear it. The policy says it uses no ad trackers.</p>
</div>

<h2>The three meters that eat beginners</h2>
<p>Six needs decay on their own clocks. Three will hurt you inside the first half hour:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Bladder</strong> — reported as the fastest meter in the game. Deal with it between tasks rather than mid-shift.</li>
  <li><strong>Hunger</strong> — the fastest of the ordinary needs; <a href="/venues/">food venues</a> have opening hours, so plan the route.</li>
  <li><strong>Fun</strong> — the one that punishes pure grinding. A danfo ride or a night out is not a distraction from the game; it <em>is</em> the economy.</li>
</ul>

<h2>What to ignore until tomorrow</h2>
<ol class="tl">
  <li><strong>Sea Plots.</strong> The visible flex, and not a first-30-minutes purchase. <a href="/sea-plots/">What they cost and do</a>.</li>
  <li><strong>Side businesses and land.</strong> Both compound, both carry a bad-day cost, and land carries the <em>omo-onile</em> levy reported as high as 35% in Ikorodu.</li>
  <li><strong>Generator, solar and inverter gear.</strong> NEPA cutting your power is a real mechanic with its own outage cue, but buying through it is a week-two decision.</li>
  <li><strong>Anything anyone offers you for real money.</strong> Game naira cannot be withdrawn to a bank account, so every "I'll pay you cash for an account / for naira" offer is a rule-break and usually a scam. Read <a href="/is-lagos-life-safe/">is Lagos Life safe?</a> before you accept a friend request.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Heat, battery and data: the phone-only problems</h2>
<p>This is a full-screen live canvas of other people walking around, so the GPU never rests. The complaints on TikTok are exactly what you would expect — phones getting hot, stuttering, and one creator's whole video being about their iPhone 12 pausing mid-game. Fixes that actually help, in order:</p>
<table>

<h2>Frequently asked</h2>

<h3>Can you install Lagos Life on a phone?</h3>
<p>Yes, but not from an app store. There is no APK, no iOS app and no Play Store listing — the site ships a web-app manifest, so you use <strong>Add to Home Screen</strong> and it launches full-screen like an app.</p>

<h3>Which iPhones can run it?</h3>
<p>iOS 16.4 or newer. Since every browser on iPhone uses the same rendering engine, changing browser will not help an old iOS — only updating will.</p>

<h3>Why does it reload on its own?</h3>
<p>The game detects failed asset loads and re-opens the page automatically, up to three times in three minutes, because a stale cached build is the normal cause.</p>

<h3>Is it free on mobile?</h3>
<p>Yes. Sign-up and play are free in any browser. It sells in-game naira top-ups, and that money cannot be withdrawn to a bank account — see <a href="/is-lagos-life-safe/">the safety page</a>.</p>

<h3>Is Lagos Life multiplayer?</h3>
<p>Real-time and shared, not a private sandbox: you see other players on the same map, chat with them, join families and squads, and take part in server-wide events such as the governor election. Which is also why it is heavier on a phone than a normal browser game.</p>

<h3>How long until it makes sense?</h3>
<p>30 minutes gets you housed and paid. Days get you the rest — see <a href="/tips/">the tips page</a> when you are there. <!-- [核实] /tips/ 决定是否要建；不建就删掉这条内链，别留 404 --></p>
